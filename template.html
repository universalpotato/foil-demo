Foil

Foil is a generalized solver for Magic: The Gathering that covers the current standard metagame, consisting of 1,968 decklists / 977 cards. Foil pilots any deck against any other deck competitively.

Magic: The Gathering is a card game with over 50 million players.

      Prior art
      

      Currently, no learned agent exists for Magic: The Gathering or games with comparable breadth of rules.
      

      The closest existing models are the small, specialized Hearthstone models from "Mastering Strategy Card Game (Hearthstone) with Improved Techniques" (Xiao et al., ByteDance, 2023), which were trained on specific card pools and beat top ladder players. Foil is a single model that plays a game with more complex rule interactions and a far larger card pool.
      


      Scaffolding
      

        

      • A full-fidelity rules engine in Rust: ~30k decisions/sec.
        • 

      • A second, clean-room rules engine for differential testing.
        • 

      


      Model
      

        

      • The model has 17M parameters and learned entirely by self-play (PPO).
        • 

      • A formerly top-1000 Mythic player on Arena spot-checked it and found it plays well.
        • 

      • It has a winning record against every frontier model we tested. A 58% winrate is comparable to the gap between top-tier and average players (in 17Lands Arena data).
        •
